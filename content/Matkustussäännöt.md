
## 🗺️ The Framework: Hex-Navigation

- **Scale:** Roughly 1–2 miles per hex.
- **Pace:**
    - **Normal:** 4 Hexes/day (Standard).
    - **Fast:** 6 Hexes/day (-5 to Perception/Navigation; higher risk of "Drifting").
    - **Slow:** 2 Hexes/day (+5 to Stealth/Foraging; "Ghost-Walk").
    - 
- **The Drift:** If the **Navigator** fails a check, the party doesn't just stop; they move into an adjacent hex they didn't intend to enter (e.g., drifting from the Forest Path into the **Nightmare Swamp**).


---

## 🎭 The Roles (GM Binder + Nightmare Twist)

Each player chooses a role for the day. Failures in these roles have "Nightmare" consequences:

1. **The Navigator (Survival):**
    - _Success:_ Moves the party toward the Temple.
    - _Failure:_ The party "Drifts" toward the Swamp (The Dreamer’s Heart) or the Mountains.

2. **The Lookout (Perception):**
    - _Success:_ Spots Echo-Walkers or Hazards before they trigger.
    - _Failure:_ The party is surprised or walks directly into a **Time-Glitched** area.

3. **The Forager (Nature/Survival):**
    - _Success:_ Finds "Safe" food/water.
    - _Failure:_ Finds **Dream-Tainted** supplies (gives -1 to Wisdom Saves for 24 hours).
    - 
4. **The Scout (Stealth/Investigation):**
    - _Success:_ Finds "Past Echoes" (Lore) or shortcuts.
    - _Failure:_ Triggers a "Looming Question" (The Nightmare Princess notices them).

## 🌲 The Biome Logic

Where they end up on your map determines the **Hazard** they face:

|Biome|The "Current Time" Threat|The "1,000-Year" Visual|
|---|---|---|
|**Forest**|**Echo-Walkers:** Stealth-based hunters.|Overgrown "Rome" ruins; White Spirit Stones.|
|**Swamp**|**Deep Sleep:** CON saves vs. Lethargy.|The Primordial Dreamer's giant gas-bubbles.|
|**Mountains**|**Broken Reality:** Gravity/Time glitches.|Floating Sentinel towers; skeletons in stone.|

---

## 🏺 The "Evidence-Based" Encounter Table

When they enter a "Feature" hex, use the system we built:

- **Visual:** A ruin they recognize from the "Past" session, but 1,000 years decayed.
- **Verbal:** A distorted "Audio Echo" of the Prayer or Sir Calen’s speech.
- **Physical:** A rusted relic (like the fishing lure or a Sentinel ring) that acts as a "Boon" or "Bane."
    

---

## ⚡ The "Session Loop"

1. **Morning:** Players pick Roles. Navigator chooses the target Hex.
2. **Travel:** Resolve 4–6 Hexes. Roll for Hazards/Features in specific hexes.
3. **Night:** A "Long Rest" check. Do they dream of the past? (Wisdom Save).
4. **The End:** Arrive at the **Temple of Dreamers** for the Elira cliffhanger.

---

## 💡 My Advice for the Prep:

Keep a **"Navigation DC"** tracker. The closer they get to the **Temple of Dreamers**, the harder the DCs become as the Nightmare gets thicker.

- **Start (Summoning):** DC 10
- **Middle (Deep Wilderness):** DC 13
- **Threshold (Temple Entrance):** DC 16
