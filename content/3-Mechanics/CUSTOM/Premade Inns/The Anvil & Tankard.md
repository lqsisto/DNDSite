**3. Inn Name:** The Anvil & Tankard

- **Inn Description:**
	- Hewn directly from the rock deep within a mountain city or stronghold.
	- Low, vaulted stone ceilings are supported by thick pillars carved with dwarven knotwork.
	- Warm light emanates from a large, central forge and numerous wall sconces.
	- Furnished with heavy stone tables and iron-banded benches.
	- Smells strongly of roasted meat, hearty ale, coal smoke, and hot metal.
	- Often filled with booming laughter and rousing drinking songs.
- **Inn Keeper Name & Description:**
	- **Borin Stonehand** (Male Mountain Dwarf).
	- Broad-shouldered, with a magnificent grey beard braided into intricate patterns and adorned with heavy silver rings.
	- His hands are calloused from years at the forge.
	- He's gruff, loud, but has a strong sense of fairness.
	- Usually wears a thick leather blacksmith's apron over well-maintained chainmail.
