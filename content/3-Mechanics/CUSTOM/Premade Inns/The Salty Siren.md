

 ### **Inn Description:**

- A rough-and-tumble dockside tavern smelling strongly of brine, cheap ale, and fried fish.
- The floor is covered in sawdust, tables are knife-scarred, and fishing nets hang from the low rafters.
- It's loud, boisterous, and constantly filled with sailors, smugglers, and dockworkers.
- Located right on the waterfront.

### **Inn Keeper Name & Description:**

- **"One-Eyed" Mog** (Male Orc).
- A hulking, green-skinned orc with a prominent eyepatch over his left eye and surprisingly few teeth.
- He has a booming laugh, wears a stained leather apron, and often settles disputes with his fists, though he's generally considered fair.
