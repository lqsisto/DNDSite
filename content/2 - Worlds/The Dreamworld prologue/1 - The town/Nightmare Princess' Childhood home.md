### Outside

#### General information
- **Senses:**

	  Small stairs lead to front door; porch with a 2 wooden chairs on them.
	  2 large windows on the left side of the door.

### Inside
##### General information
- **Senses:**
	- **Sight**:  It's dark, but there's enough light coming through the windows so you can see
	  A small room where it looks like there lived at least 4 people.
	  On the corner there are 4 beds, 2 big and 2 small. 
	  Next the beds is a toybox.
	  A cold fire place is in the far end of the room **-> The fireplace lights up when players enter the room**
	  Near the windows is a big wooden dining table and 2 benches along side it.
	  Middle of the room is a **drawer with a music box on it -> Clearly broken down - shimmers green light from the inside**
	- **Smell**: Dust, wood, wet

##### Activities within the room
***Fix the music box***
- **Search (Investigation or Perception DC 13):** Find 3 missing music box pieces hidden throughout the house (e.g., under bed, in a toy chest, behind a photo frame).

- **Final Check – Tinker with the mechanism (Sleight of Hand or Intelligence DC 14):** Carefully fit the parts together and wind it.
