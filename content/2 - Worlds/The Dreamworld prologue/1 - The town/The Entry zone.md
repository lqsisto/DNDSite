### Location 2 - The Descent

#### 🌀 Stage 1 – The Forking Path

- **Left – Mirror Hall:**  
	Shattered mirrors reflect altered selves.  
	→ WIS Save DC 12 (Overwhelmed or gain strange memory)
	
- **Right – Whisper Door:**  
	Voices whisper players' names behind a sealed door.  
	→ INT Check DC 10 (Recognize future NPC voice)

---

#### 🏠 Stage 2 – Tilted House

- **Kitchen (Time Freeze):**  
	Floating objects mid-air, time unfreezes violently.  
	→ DEX Save DC 11 (Stylish dodge or just a scare)
	
- **Crawlspace Garden:**  
	Colorless roses, one glowing green.  
	→ Insight DC 13 (One rose watches them)

---

#### 🌫️ Stage 3 – Leap of Faith

- Staircase ends in swirling mist. Floating doorway ahead.  
	→ No check: Players must jump. Mist carries them to dreamtown.
