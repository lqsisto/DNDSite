## Locations

### The green house
##### DM notes
- *Anything that might change because of other variables within area. Or something else worth mentioning. FOR DMs eyes only*

##### General information

**The green house come to life when players enter it.
Before waking up:

- **Senses:**
	- **Smell:** dirt, mold
	- **Sight:** A big, dirty greenhouse. The mold and moss slowly grow higher and higher.
	  All the plants are wild and most of them are dead
	  Broken glass from the roof is laying around
	- **Movement**: Water slowly drops from the pipes that run high in the air


**When the greenhouse repairs itself

- **Senses:**
	- **Smell**: The multitude of smells attack the players, mixed with fertilizer, and moist ground
	- **Sight**: 
		- Thousands different plants, all growing neatly in their own place.
		- In the middle of the room there is a big valve.
		- You notice that the greenhouse doors are closed **-> cannot open the doors**



**VERY SOON**

Players hear a big cracking noise and hissing.
The big pipe in the middle of room starts first leaking slowly, and then bursts open. The water starts to fill up the room.


##### Activities within the room
### 💡 Puzzle Steps:

### 🔁 Mechanics:

#### 🕒 Timed Challenge:

- The room will fill in **3 rounds**.
	
- Each round, the water level rises. At the end of round 3, players are submerged (no one dies, but they’ll be “reset” to outside and miss the clocktower piece unless they retry).

#### 🔍 1. **Find the Valve**

- **Investigation or Perception (DC 12)** to spot the metal hatch in the floor near the vines.
	
- (Optional): Arcana or Nature (DC 12) reveals the greenhouse is reacting to **emotional memory**, not just physical presence.
	

#### 🌿 2. **Clear the Overgrowth**

- The valve is tangled in magical vines pulsing with green light.
	
- Players must:
	
	- **Slash/Cut** the vines (Athletics DC 13), OR
		
	- **Calm the greenhouse** by mimicking care (Nature, Performance, or Persuasion DC 13).
		

#### 🔧 3. **Turn the Valve**

- The valve is jammed. A successful **Strength (Athletics DC 14)** or **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand DC 15)** check shuts it off in time.
	
- Optional: Aid another to lower DC by 2.
- When the valve turns, water recedes unnaturally fast. The greenhouse fades back into ruin, but **a glowing vine unfurls from the wall**, wrapping around a piece of the **clocktower gear** and presenting it to the players.


#### **💀 4. If players fail to turn the valve
- Feeling of drowning
- They wake up standing soaking wet, with one of the keys for the [[clocktower]] next to them
