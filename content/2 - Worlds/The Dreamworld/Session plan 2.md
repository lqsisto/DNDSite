
### Goal/Stakes
- Players go into the past to learn that the summoning braziers were built long, long time ago and that the Nightmare Princess has ruled the world for centuries.


### The Fronts (What NPCs want)
- Sir Calen Dreth: Wants to prove his worth to Nightmare Princess by finishing the construction
- Store owners in the town: Want the construction finished because they are exhausted to provide all materials
- [[Sisar Arelen Lyra]] wants the brazier demolished. It's unholy and she's afraid of it.
- [[Innkeeper Cilia Tolla]] Has mixed feelings. The construction brought people to town and money in her pocket, but she knows [[Sisar Arelen Lyra]] well and is worried about her.
- Regular townspeople: Are excited about the brazier. They want to see their loved ones.

### The Locations
- #### Everhut
	- Vibe: 
		- Cozy. Smells like tea, fresh food and thyme. Looks like it was built inside a tree. There are multiple trapdoors on each floor that take you to different rooms.
	- Detail:
		- In armory players can choose any basic weapon armor
		- In study something similar but for mages
	- Clue:
		- There are multiple items that could belong to the [[Kutsuja]]
- #### Millwater Inn:
	- Vibe:
		- Cozy, crowded. People everywhere, yelling, laughter, tobacco smoke and mead
	- Detail:
		- All food made here is EXTREMELY GOOD for some reason...
	- Clue:
		- [[Innkeeper Cilia Tolla]] has a small book behind the counter she's written down names of all Ivory Sentinels who have started acting weird.
		- [[Sisar Arelen Lyra]] is worried about the construction
- #### Millwater Lumbermill
	- Vibe:
		- Busy, strict. The foreman [[Heswyse 'Hes' Ylofyx Rosikini]] is screaming instructions to workers 
	- Detail:
		- There's a saw blade similar to lumbermills next to the river but the saw blade moves on its own 
	- Clue:
		- The workers are complaining how the whole forest has been cut down for the supports of the brazier
	- Action Point:
		- Saw blade suddenly comes off and shoots up to one of the workers. Players need to heal one of the workers [[Millwater Lumbermill accident]]
- #### Millwater Bakery
	- Vibe:
		- It smells like bread and fresh pastries. Low humming can be heard from the backroom.
	- Detail:
		- The owner, [[Garuk Doughfist]] decorates everything according to orcish tradition.
	- Clue:
		- Some fliers on the counter inviting everyone for the "big revelation party"
- #### Millwater Temple
	- Vibe:
		- **Senses:** Smell of incense trying to cover the smell of moist ground, voices echo in empty space
		- **Description:** Big, cold, and dark stone building. Lots of lights inside to make it more comfortable, but the result is still not too nice
	- Detail:
		- The fountain on the middle of the room is similar with what players saw in [[Temple Complex of Summoning]]
	- Clue:
		- [[Sisar Arelen Lyra]] is praying [[Melna, Veden Jumalatar]] and crying. Ever after the construction started, Melna has been harder to reach.
