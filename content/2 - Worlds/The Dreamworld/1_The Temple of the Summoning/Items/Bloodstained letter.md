## **📕 Description**
No time.

If you're reading this, then she did it.  
You’ve arrived.

I don’t know what you remember. Maybe nothing.  
That’s normal. That’s how the Verge keeps you quiet.

It was her — the Inviter.  
She leads us — what's left of us. She's still fighting. She brought you here.

She believed you could help. That you’re different. 
I hope she was right.

I am Eris Vale.  
One of hers. The Watcher of the First Gate.  
I helped open the way for you. I bought time.

The Altar of Summoning behind you — it won’t let you die easily. None of them won't. 
You’ll wake again. And again. But it marks you.  
A scar each time. Small — but it grows. We don’t know why.  
They say too many deaths... change you.

They started killing us. The Ivory Sentinels.  
They used to protect us. Now they hunt us. I don’t know why.

They are close by. I can already hear them.

The Town of Summoning is just ahead. Ruined. Start there. Look for the signs.

You’ll see the paths soon - they lead you to another temple. That’s where she is.  

She’s waiting for you.

Just— stay together. Trust each other.  
And if anything wears your face…

Run.

I’m out of time.

— Eris  
Watch the flames.
