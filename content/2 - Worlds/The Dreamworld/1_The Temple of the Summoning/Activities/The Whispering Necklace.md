


Attached to a broken statue [[The Shattered Statue – A Forgotten Figure]]

- Players will get the necklace when they return the eyes into the statue
- A **small stone pendant**, carved with **the same green-eye motif** seen throughout the ruins.
- When held, it feels **warm and alive**, and the wearer occasionally hears **soft whispers** in an unknown language.
- **Mechanical Effect:** It doesn’t grant power, but it lets the wearer sense **the presence of dream-touched creatures** nearby. If the players keep it, it may react in strange ways later in the campaign.
