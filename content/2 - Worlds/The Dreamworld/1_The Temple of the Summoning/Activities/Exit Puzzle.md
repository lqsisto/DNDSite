### **Exit Puzzle**

A shimmering **green magical barrier** blocks the only way out of the temple. The players cannot pass through it unless they find the **correct object** hidden somewhere in the ruins.

#### **The Barrier’s Appearance & Interaction**

- When players approach, the barrier **hums softly**, and **tiny green runes pulse** across its surface.
- If a player tries to pass through, they feel **intense resistance**—like trying to push through thick water—but are ultimately repelled.
- Testing with objects (throwing a rock, using weapons) shows that **non-living things pass through, but living creatures do not**.

[[The Dreamer’s Talisman]]

#### **Hints for Players to Find the Talisman:**

- **A faint green glow** occasionally flickers from its hiding spot.
- A **whispering wind** guides players toward it if they listen carefully.
- The **Dreaming Guardian** may subtly point toward it or linger nearby.
- An **inscription near the barrier** reads: _"Only those who walk with the Dream may leave this place."_

#### **Solution & Outcome:**

- Once a player **touches the barrier while holding the talisman**, it **parts like mist**, allowing passage.
- The talisman does **not disappear**, meaning the players can take it with them—it may **react strangely** in future encounters with dream magic.
