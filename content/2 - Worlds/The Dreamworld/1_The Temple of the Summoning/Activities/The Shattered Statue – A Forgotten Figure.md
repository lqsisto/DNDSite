- In the **center of the ruins**, players can find the remains of a **large stone statue**, now broken into pieces.
- A **DC 14 Investigation (Int) check** lets them notice that:
	- The **eyes of the statue once had something embedded in them—perhaps gemstones or something else removed long ago.**
	- There’s **an inscription on the base**, barely legible:_
		- _"Only the sleepers may pass through."_



## The lost eyes of the statue
- Green diamonds
- The eyes first pull towards each other, and then towards the statue

### The first lost eye
- Located in the [[The Temple of reflections]], next to the [[Exit Puzzle#**The Dreamer’s Talisman**]]

### The second lost eye
- In one of the buildings next to the marketplace. Found fairly easily
