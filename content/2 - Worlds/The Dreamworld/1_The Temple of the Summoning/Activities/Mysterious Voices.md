- As the players explore, an occasional gust of wind moves through the ruins, carrying **whispers**—soft, almost like a forgotten memory.
- If a player succeeds on a **DC 12 Perception (Wis) check**, they can make out part of the whisper:
	- _"Why did they leave us...?"_
	- _"She said it would last forever..."_
	- _"Green flames... green flames..."_
	- _"The castle... the castle is waiting..."_
