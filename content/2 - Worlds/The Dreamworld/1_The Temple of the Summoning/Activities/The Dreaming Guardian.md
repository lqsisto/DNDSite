### **2. A Non-Combat Encounter – The Dreaming Guardian**

A presence lingers in the temple, but it is not hostile—at least, not yet.

- Players hear **soft footsteps on stone** or see **faint glowing eyes** watching from the shadows, but no creature appears.
- If they stay long enough, they encounter a **Dreaming Guardian**—a translucent, spectral figure in ancient robes.
- The guardian **does not speak directly**, but instead communicates through gestures, illusions, or whispers in their minds.
- If players are respectful, the guardian might show them **a memory of the temple’s past**—a grand hall, filled with people, before it was ruined.
- If players attempt to **damage or steal anything**, the guardian **glares at them**, and they feel a wave of cold dread, like something terrible is watching them.
