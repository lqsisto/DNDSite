- When players investigate the ruins, they notice faded symbols and carvings across the stone surfaces—circles, spirals, and flowing patterns, many of which seem to focus around the **color green**.

- A **DC 15 History (Int)** check reveals:
    - Green is significant to this place, not just as decoration but as a **symbol of power, presence, or control**.
    - Certain patterns in the ruins appear to represent **eyes**, always glowing green, always watching.
    - A strange **phrase in an ancient dialect** is carved into a broken pillar. A successful check or Comprehend Languages reveals something like:
        - _"The green flame never fades."_
        - _"Beware the one who whispers in the dark."_
        - _"To wake is to forget. To forget is to be free."_
