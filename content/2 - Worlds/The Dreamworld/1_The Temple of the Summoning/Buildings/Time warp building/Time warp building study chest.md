### **Level 3 Spells**

1. **Counterspell** – Interrupt a spell being cast.
	
2. **Fireball** – Classic area damage spell; 8d6 fire in a 20-foot radius.
	
3. **Hypnotic Pattern** – Charm creatures in a 30-foot cube.
	
4. **Fly** – Grant flight for 10 minutes.
	
5. **Major Image** – Create an illusion that includes sound, smell, and temperature.

---

### **Level 4 Spells**

6. **Greater Invisibility** – Target remains invisible even after attacking or casting.
	
7. **Dimension Door** – Teleport yourself and one willing creature up to 500 feet.
	
8. **Polymorph** – Transform a creature into a beast (or vice versa).
	
9. **Phantasmal Killer** – Terrify a creature with an illusory nightmare.
	
10. **Wall of Fire** – Create a blazing wall that deals damage to those near it.

---

### **Level 5 Spells**

11. **Wall of Force** – Invisible, nearly indestructible wall.
	
12. **Cloudkill** – Creates a poisonous fog that moves each round.
	
13. **Modify Memory** – Rewrite up to 10 minutes of someone's memories.
	
14. **Teleportation Circle** – Create a temporary portal to a known sigil location.
	
15. **Geas** – Magically compel a creature to follow a command for up to 30 days.

---

### **Level 6 Spells**

16. **Disintegrate** – Deal 10d6 + 40 force damage; reduces target to dust if killed.
	
17. **True Seeing** – Grants vision through illusions, invisibility, and into the Ethereal Plane.
	
18. **Mass Suggestion** – Suggest a course of action to up to 12 creatures.

---

### **Level 7 Spells**

19. **Plane Shift** – Travel to another plane of existence (or banish unwilling targets there).

---

### **Level 8 Spell**

20. **Feeblemind** – Crush a creature’s intellect and charisma, potentially ruining spellcasters.
