## Locations

### The centre of the circle

##### General information
- **Senses**
	- **Sight**: 
		- Your gear next to you
		- Circular broken building that's ancient. 
		- Rubble on the floor. 
		- Middle of the room a brazier. 
				-> The brazier looks like it's breathing. A green light under it.
		- You can see the sun shining through the cracks of the wall.
		- There are symbols and markings on the floor and on the walls. 
			 -> **DC 15 History(int)**   
							- Green is significant to this place, not just as decoration but as a **symbol of power, presence, or control**.
							- Certain patterns in the ruins appear to represent **eyes**, always glowing green
							- A strange **phrase in an ancient dialect** is carved into a broken pillar. A successful check or Comprehend Languages reveals:
									-   _"The green flame never fades."_
	- **Sounds**: Birds, wind

##### Activities within the room
**1. Looking outside**
 You realize you are in an ancient city that's taken over by nature.
 The city is built on the some kind of natural platform.

- North: A big building that has 2 floors. Looking at the shape it reminds of church. [[Temple of reflections]]
- East: One of the few buildings still standing [[MuralBuilding]]. This is the second biggest building that is still standing
- South: Multiple partly collapsed houses. Some are still standing. There's also something that seems to be an ancient marketplace.
- West: A green smoky wall made of thick green smoke. Behind it a forest that seems to continue forever


2. **The castle**
+ In the fat north, there is a castle built on the mountain wall. There's smoke coming from the castle.
