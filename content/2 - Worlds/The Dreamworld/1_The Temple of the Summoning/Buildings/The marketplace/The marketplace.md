## Locations

### The main market area
##### DM notes

##### General information
- **Big picture:**
	- A market place with 12 stone stands.
- **Details:**
	- Area is big and rectangle shaped
	- Trees and roots are growing through the stone pavements
	- It feels peaceful, sun feels warm on your face
- **Interactables**:
	-  A broken water fountain in the middle of the marketplace with a tree fallen on top. 
	 **You can hear the water splashing even when you clearly see it's broken**

	- A broken statue in the back. 
	  **The sun still paints the shadow on ground**


##### Activities within the room
###### The Fountain
- On the fountain you find the same coins that are in the fountain on the [[Temple of reflections]] garden
- Someone has scratched the word "*IVORY SENTINELS*" on the stone





###### The Statue
- The statue only has one eye. It's Green diamond. It vibrates gently. When players touch it pulls towards the [[Temple of reflections]]
