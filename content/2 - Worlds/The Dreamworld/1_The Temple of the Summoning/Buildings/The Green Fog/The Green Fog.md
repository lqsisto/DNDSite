## General features
> The fog looks like thick smoke, with color of green in it. It moves slowly and peacefully 
- **Senses:**
	- Touch: Feels slightly moist, leaves a green hint to hands if touched
	- If watched for long enough, you'll start to feel a bit dizzy


## Locations


### The wall
##### DM notes
- To pass through the fog, players need to find the [[The Dreamer’s Talisman]].
- If players try to walk towards the smoke, it pushes the person back with equal force.
- **If inviter has not appeared yet, she appears when the green fog is opened**

- **WHEN PLAYERS HAVE THE TALISMAN:**
	- The fog vanishes slowly.

###  Behind the wall
- Ancient path, almost completely covered by grass continue onward.
