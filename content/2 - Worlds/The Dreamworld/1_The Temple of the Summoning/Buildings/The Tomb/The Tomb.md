## General features
#### Ceilings
High on rooms, 3ft on corridors

#### Doors
Made of some kind of metal, very heavy

#### Walls
Dark marble that's very skillfully done by hand

#### Floors
Cracked stone floor, some plants are growing between the stones

#### Light
Green lanterns are burning everywhere in the dungeon. Can see without darkvision.

## Locations

### Room 1 - Entrance

##### General information
- **Senses:**
	- **Sight**: Stone floor, walls made from dark marble. Green light fills the room, there are 2 lanterns on the both sides of the room.
	  On the middle, there's 2 pillars and two graves; One pillar has fallen down and crashed on graves.
	  There are 3 statues on the other side of the room. The one on the middle is holding its hand up as gesturing to stop.
	  Behind the statues there are 2 closed doors.
	- **Smell**: damp, thick, something strong comes the coffins in the middle
	- **Sounds**: You hear absolutely nothing. It is dead quiet. Almost too quiet...
	- **Danger**: you feel worried

##### Activities within the room
**1. Investigating the coffins.**
	- Inside the **coffins are 2 skeletons**. With both of them is **bottle of some strongly smelling liquid**. 
	
**2. A puzzle with the doors.**
	- Players need to **walk through the door** and the hallway behind it **backwards** in order to go through. Otherwise they **come out from the door they didn't go in**. 
	When approached to middle statue, the statue says with hollow voice:

> [!readaloud] 
*I am the Warden of Silent Stone,  
Guardian of those whose names are gone.  
Strike at me, and steel shall sing—  
Take what is not yours, and I shall bring  
Judgment swift, no plea, no stay—  
Grave robbers shall not walk away.*
>
*To continue now, heed ancient art,  
Where steps retreat, the journey can start.  
What once was forward may lead you astray,  
But wisdom walks in an opposite way.*


### Room 2 - Hallway

##### General information
- **Senses:**
	- *Sight*: Long hallway, on the right side you can see it's collapsed on the far end. There are three paths that all continue in the same direction you're going
	- *Smell*: From the farthest turn, you can smell something sweet and sour... and rotten
	- *Sounds*: Only your footsteps

##### Activities within the room
