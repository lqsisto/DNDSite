- **Early Campaign (Mysterious Guide)**
- 
	- First appears as a traveler, a storyteller, or even a **merchant selling odd trinkets** (some of which may be useful later).
	- Leaves cryptic messages in places the party explores—written notes, whispers in the wind, or strange visions.
	- Might give small nudges like:
		- “The road ahead is twisted, but those who listen will find the way.”
		- “Green eyes watch you, but not all mean harm.”
- **Mid-Campaign (More Direct Influence)**
	
	- Starts appearing in dreams or visions, offering advice in moments of rest.
	- May intervene _once_ if the party is truly lost, but in a subtle way—perhaps sending a green-eyed raven to lead them.
	- Hints that the Dreamworld is not what it seems. (Example: “You ever notice how the stars never change?”)
	- If the party is struggling with their identity, they may say: “Your names... do you remember them? Hold on to them.”
- **Late Campaign (Final Meeting & Reveal)**
	
	- After the stronghold is destroyed, the Inviter meets them fully revealed—no more disguises.
	- They explain that the Dreamworld is fading for them and that the only way out is the cliff.
	- They hesitate before pushing them—not out of cruelty, but because they genuinely enjoyed their time together.
	- **Last Words Before the Push:**
		- “I’m sorry, but you don’t belong here. You never did.”
		- “One last step, my friends. I promise you’ll wake up.”
		- “This world will forget you, but I won’t. If you ever dream of green eyes again... you’ll know where to find me.”
- **The Letter After Waking Up**
	
	- Confirms that what they did _mattered_.
	- Hints that the Nightmare Princess is still a threat.
	- Leaves the door open for them to return someday

- What if the Inviter _can’t_ wake up like the players can? What if they are forever bound to the Dreamworld?
