## **📕 Description**
Beautiful shortsword made from [[Kalmarauta]]. It's white and looks like porselain. The handle is made from leather and is mostly broken.
The blade glows surprisingly bright, even after lying centuries in grave

---

## **🌟 Abilities & Effects**
- **Activated Effect:** Once per day, when hit, it emits a single clear chime that silences all mundane noise in a 30-foot radius for 1 minute (magic speech/sound unaffected). This ties to its “hymn” theme.
