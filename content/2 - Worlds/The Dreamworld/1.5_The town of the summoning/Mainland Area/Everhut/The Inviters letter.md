_My friends,_

If you are reading this, then you have made it to the Everhut. Do not be afraid — it is of my making, drawn from the dream itself. Within these walls, you are safe. Here, nothing can harm you.

But first, you must understand: this is not the world you know. You walk in the plane of dreams, in the place called the **[[Unen Reuna]]**, a realm born of slumber and memory, where reality bends to the will of those who can dream.

Once, long ago, there was a girl named **Eleonora**. She was — and still is — cursed to sleep without end. When someone sleeps long enough, they learn to shape their dreams, and she discovered she could shape entire worlds within hers. In the beginning, she filled this place with wonder — a haven of her own creation, a paradise where she could escape her imprisonment.

But eternity wears the spirit thin. What began in hope turned to bitterness. Her kindness withered into cruelty, her creations into monsters. She calls herself the **Nightmare Princess**, and it was she who corrupted the **Ivory Sentinels**, who once guarded the cities and temples of this world.

And then there are the Empty Ones — her accidental creations. Mindless monsters, copies of creatures who died near the altars of summoning. In this realm, few things are truly real; when illusions are forced into reality, they shatter, giving birth to these abominations. That is how the Empty Ones came to be.

And yet, she can be resisted. West from here lies the **Temple of the Dreamers**, one of the last places not yet fully bound to her. If you reach it, her hold here will weaken.

There are three paths that may lead you there:

- The **mountains** — where food is scarce and survival is a test of endurance. Danger from living creatures is rare, but the land itself is harsh.
	
- The **forest** — where the Dream folds in upon itself, and even your own steps may betray you. This is the Forest of the Ancients, one of Eleonora’s first creations, along with the Temple of Summoning, where you awoke.
	
- The **swamp** — where despair seeps into your bones and whispers that each step may be your last. Here, fragments of her dreams go to die.
	

Each path has its dangers, yet each can carry you onward.

If you have not met **Eris**, you must. He bears truths I cannot trust to parchment. He will be your guide. I sent him to meet you before the green mist that blocks the temple’s entry. He carries a talisman that allows passage, and he should let you through once you arrive.

Rest now, while you may. The Everhut will open to you always, so long as my strength endures. Beyond its door, you are in danger. She will soon know you are here, and she will send her Sentinels to hunt you. Be careful, and stay safe. I will meet you in the Temple of the Dreamers.

_Elira, the one who invited you here_

_Post Scriptum: I apologize for my trick with the pool inside the Temple of Reflections. Taking you to the very edge of this reality was the only way to be certain it was truly you._

Watch the flames.
