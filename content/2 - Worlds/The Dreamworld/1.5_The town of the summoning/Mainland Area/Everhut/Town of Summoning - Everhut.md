- **Name & Type:** Everhut

### From the outside
- **First Impression:**
	- Looks like a soap bubble; slightly transparent
	- The bubble slowly moves and distorts anything behind it
	- MOOD: Confusion

- **Why It Matters:** The entrance to Everhut


### From the inside
- **First Impression:**
	- Small, Cozy house. Looks like it's inside a tree
	- Fire in the fireplace can be heard, shadows move on the walls
	- MOOD: home
- **Activities:**
	1. Letter on the table next to the fireplace [[The Inviters letter]]

	2. Blacksmith's forge
		1. - **Repair broken gear**: If enemies shatter shields or armor, players can fix them here instead of buying replacements.
		2. **Sharpen blades**: A successful Smith’s Tools check could grant a weapon **+1 to damage rolls until the next long rest** (minor but fun buff).

	3. The magic study
		1. **Research Spells or Lore**: Wizards can copy spells into their spellbook (rules for cost/time are in PHB). Any class can use the study to research clues about your world.
		2. **Identify items without Identify spell**: Arcana checks in a proper study could work as a substitute.
		3. **Temporary Buffs**: Studying here before a long rest could give inspiration or grant advantage on the next Arcana/Investigation check.
		4. **Potion Brewing / Scroll Scribing**: If they have materials, allow downtime crafting of scrolls or potions
