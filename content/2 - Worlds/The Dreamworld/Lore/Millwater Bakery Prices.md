

### Everyday Breads

- **Simple Loaf** (dark rye or barley) – **2 Whirls (2 cp)**  
    Filling, slightly sour, keeps well on the road.
    
- **Soft White Bread** – **3 Whirls (3 cp)**  
    Rare luxury for travelers; kids love it.
    
- **Seeded Hearth Bread** – **4 Whirls (4 cp)**  
    Heavy, nutty, famously “sticks to your ribs.”
    

---

### Local Treats

- **Millcakes** – **6 Whirls (6 cp)**  
    Dense oat cakes with dried berries; lumberworkers swear by them.
    
- **Stone-Oven Twists** – 8 Whirls (**8 cp**)  
    Braided bread sticks, crispy outside, soft inside. Addictive.
    

---

### Orcish Specialties (Goruk’s Pride)

A quiet subversion of expectations.

- **Night-Risen Dough** – **10 Shards, 5 Whirls  (1 sp 5 cp)**  
    Fermented overnight. Slightly warm when torn open.  
    Locals say it “tastes better if you don’t ask how it’s made.”
