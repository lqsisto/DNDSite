### **Shared Ranks (All Initiates)**

1. **Shard-Bound** – New recruit, bound to the order by ritual and oath.
	
2. **Ceramic Acolyte** – Initiate trained in basic temple rites and combat.

---

### **Spiritual Path – Clerics**

3. **Votive Adept** – Learns sacred texts, dream-binding, and basic healing.
	
4. **Echo-Keeper** – Interprets divine riddles and ancient echoes of the Dreamworld.
	
5. **Chalice-Bearer** – Conducts sacred rites; protects spiritual sanctity of temples.
	
6. **Verdant Voice** – Channels the Dreamworld's will through visions and whispers.
	
7. **Mirror-Soul** – Capable of divine illusions and memory restoration.
	
8. **Dreambinder** – Guides spirits, seals nightmares, anchors sanctums.
	
9. **Porcelain Prophet** – Speaks with the voice of the Ivory Sentinels’ divine authority.

---

### **Fighter Path – Paladins**

3. **Obsidian Arm** – First to train with the faction’s enchanted weaponry.
	
4. **Hollow Guard** – Guardian of temples and ancient roads.
	
5. **Warden of Echoes** – Silent protector; wears full ceremonial armor.
	
6. **Greenfire Sentinel** – Wields magic-touched weaponry to fight nightmare creatures.
	
7. **Ivory Wall** – Leads squads, defends against invaders in sacred grounds.
	
8. **Sanctum Champion** – Duelist and commander of battlefield operations.
	
9. **Paragon of the Veil** – Final rank; protector of the veil between dream and waking.
