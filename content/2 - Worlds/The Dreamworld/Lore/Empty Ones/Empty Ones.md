
## **📌 Summary**
> *Empty ones are soulless, heartless creatures that once were humans. The [[Painajaisten Prinsessa]] owns them and they obey her. No one knows how or why she controls them.*

## **📜 Detailed Description**
*When the [[Painajaisten Prinsessa]] built her world, she accidentally created the empty ones too. [[Heräämisen temppelit]] were designed to make sure that no one in her eternal dream ever wakes up, but something went wrong with her magic, or perhaps she didn't care after all, but it turned out that everyone who died inside the reach of the awakening temple, were turned into... Monsters*.

- If a player dies near the temple of awakening, an empty one is created.
- It's copy of the character with exact same stats
