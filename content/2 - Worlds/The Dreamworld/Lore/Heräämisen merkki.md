## 🩸 **Lore of the Death Scars – "Marks of the Echo"**

> _"To return from death is no gift. It is an echo. And echoes always leave behind traces."_  
> — Fragment of an Ivory Sentinel scroll, found scorched near a ruined temple

### ✴️ Origin:

The **Temples of Summoning** were once sanctuaries of soul-binding — ancient sites created by the **[[Kalvasvartio]]**) to trap **echoes of dying souls** within the Dreamworld. They were later **corrupted or reconfigured** by the **Nightmare Princess**, who discovered how to use these echoes to bind memory, emotion, and pain into physical form.

The **"brazier"** in each temple doesn’t just restore a body — it **summons a person back into the dream** using a tether forged by their dying essence.

But each time a character dies and returns…

> They are not **perfectly restored.**  
> Something is lost. And something is marked.

---

### 🔥 The Scar Mechanic ("Marks of the Echo"):

Each time a character dies and is reborn by a **Temple of Summoning**, they gain a **new scar** somewhere on their body. The scar:

- Is **cold to the touch**, regardless of environment
- Is **permanent** — even magical healing or restoration cannot remove it
- Sometimes **burns faintly** when near other Echo-bound beings (like [[Empty Ones]])

---

### 🕯️ What the Scars _Actually_ Are (Secret Lore for the DM):

Each scar is a **fragment of their original soul that didn’t return**, sealed within the summoning circle. This:

- **Weakens** their connection to the Dreamworld
- Makes it easier for the Nightmare Princess (or other dream entities) to **influence** or observe them
- Is what gives rise to **Empty Ones** — the hollow remnants made from what was left behind
- The **more a character dies**, the more "unwhole" they become. Eventually, if one dies too many times…
	- Their **soul cannot return**
	- Or worse: they return, but **start turning into something else**
