- All NPCs have a mark that they belong to the [[Dreamworld]]. [[The Green Mark of Dreamworld]] 
- The dream is a dream. If players wonder too far off, they will automatically get back to the path they left from
- **Recurring Landmarks** – They can try to leave, but no matter which direction they take, they end up at the same ruins, tree, or road.

- Since everything in this world has bright green eyes or features, you can use them as subtle signposts:
	- **Guiding Lights** – Fireflies, glowing runes, or green-eyed ravens always lead them back to the correct path.

- **Déjà Vu Moments** – "Haven’t we passed this place before?" or NPCs seem to repeat conversations.
- **Fading Memories** – Players may find notes or objects that _should_ remind them of who they are—but the details slip away.
- **Glitches in the World** – Trees moving when unobserved, roads that change direction when they look back.
