## 🎚️ **Scaling Difficulty**
- Adjust the **number of enemies** (1 elite foe, 2-3 normal, or a horde of weak enemies).
- Add **unique effects** (a teleporting enemy, one that steals memories, or a boss with lair actions).
- If non-combat, increase the **complexity of puzzles or social encounters**.

---

### 🛠️ **Customizing for Locations**
- **Forest:** Add eerie animal behavior, shifting paths, or tree spirits.  
- **Mountain:** Strong winds, crumbling paths, or echoes leading to false directions.  
- **Ruins/Town:** NPCs who "glitch," haunted buildings, or doors that lead to impossible locations.  
- **Castle:** Moving staircases, cursed weapons, or animated suits of armor that only attack in darkness.
