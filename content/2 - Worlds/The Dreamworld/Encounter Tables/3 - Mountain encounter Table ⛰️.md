## ⛰️ **Mountain Path Encounters** (Levels 5-7)
| d12  | Encounter | Type | Difficulty (CR) | Notes |
|------|----------|------|----------------|-------|
| 1  | **Avalanche Spirits** | Hazard / Combat | CR 5 | Ghostly figures **trigger an avalanche**, forcing players into a fight or a skill challenge to survive. |
| 2  | **Cracked Reality** | Environmental | – | The world **glitches**, showing the Dreamworld’s unstable nature. Players may glimpse the real world or hear voices. |
| 3  | **Frozen Undead** | Combat | CR 6 | Coldwatch soldiers, trapped in the Dreamworld for centuries, rise as **wights** or **specters**. |
| 4  | **Climbing Challenge** | Puzzle / Hazard | – | Players must scale a dangerous cliff. STR/DEX (DC 16) or magic required. |
| 5  | **Shattered Guardian** | Combat | CR 6-7 | A golem or construct **malfunctions**, mistaking the party for invaders. |
| 6  | **The Inviter’s Message** | Social | – | A glowing sigil appears, delivering a cryptic message from the **Inviter**. |
| 7  | **Echo of the Nightmare Princess** | Social / Combat | CR 6 | A shimmering illusion of the **Nightmare Princess** questions the players. If disrespected, she summons shadows. |
| 8  | **Time Loop Trap** | Puzzle | – | Players experience **the same moment repeating** until they find the cause. |
| 9  | **Stormborn Beasts** | Combat | CR 5-7 | A pair of **Dream Griffons** with lightning-infused wings attack. |
| 10 | **Frozen Guardian** | Combat | CR 6 | A **sleeping frost giant** awakens, demanding answers. |
| 11 | **Veil Between Worlds** | Social / Puzzle | – | A shimmering wall separates two realities. Players may see themselves **as they were before entering the Dreamworld**. |
| 12 | **Skyborne Horror** | Combat | CR 7 | A **massive winged nightmare** (Wyvern-like but dream-infused) swoops down to attack. |
