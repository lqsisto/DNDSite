# 🎲 Random Encounter Table

### **How to Use:**
- Roll a **d12** when an encounter is needed.
- Adjust difficulty based on **player level**.
- Some encounters are **combat**, others are **puzzles, environmental effects, or social**.
- For level-appropriate encounters, use the **CR scaling** guide in the table.

---

## 🌲 **Forest of the Ancients Encounters** (Levels 3-5)
| d12  | Encounter | Type | Difficulty (CR) | Notes |
|------|----------|------|----------------|-------|
| 1  | **Whispers in the Trees** | Environmental | – | Players hear eerie voices calling them deeper. The whispers offer secrets but lead them astray. |
| 2  | **Phantom Stag** | Social / Combat | CR 3-5 | A ghostly stag with glowing green eyes watches the party. It tests their worthiness, attacking if threatened. |
| 3  | **Root Trap** | Puzzle / Hazard | – | The ground shifts, trapping players in **grasping roots**. STR (DC 14) or magic needed to escape. |
| 4  | **Dream Eaters** (2-3 Shadow Hounds) | Combat | CR 4-5 | These creatures feed on memories, **causing players to forget** spells or abilities until defeated. |
| 5  | **Fey Tricksters** | Social | – | Mischievous fey creatures (Pixies or Quicklings) demand a riddle contest or play pranks. |
| 6  | **Summoning Residue** | Puzzle | – | The air shimmers as a failed summoning ritual reactivates. Players must **interrupt the magic** before something breaks through. |
| 7  | **Living Nightmare** (1 Nightmare Husk) | Combat | CR 5 | A humanoid figure wrapped in shadows attacks, **warping reality** around it. Killing it causes visions of the Nightmare Princess. |
| 8  | **Watcher in the Fog** | Environmental | – | A distant figure with glowing green eyes **watches** but does not approach. If chased, it vanishes. |
| 9  | **Collapsed Path** | Hazard / Puzzle | – | A bridge or path has crumbled. Players must **find another route** or repair it. |
| 10 | **Echoes of the Past** | Social / Exploration | – | The players see **ghostly figures** reenacting an event. Interacting may reveal secrets. |
| 11 | **The Twisted Guardian** | Combat | CR 4-6 | A **corrupted treant** blocks the path, demanding tribute or fighting intruders. |
| 12 | **Portal Malfunction** | Puzzle | – | A shimmering portal flickers in and out of existence. Where does it lead? Players can stabilize it or risk jumping through. |

---





### 🎲 **Scaling Difficulty**
- For **weaker parties**, remove creatures or reduce numbers.
- For **stronger parties**, add **lair actions, legendary resistances, or secondary effects** (e.g., Dream Eaters causing exhaustion).
