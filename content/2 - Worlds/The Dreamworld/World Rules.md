### 🌙 **Dreamworld & Narrative Rules**

1. **The Dreamworld is a constructed reality** — surreal, symbolic, and semi-fluid, formed from the memories, emotions, and history of the Nightmare Princess.
	
2. **The world is not fully stable** — landmarks, trees, and terrain may shift or repeat (deja vu moments).
	
3. **Temples, ruins, and certain locations** are anchored to the Dreamworld more strongly and remain consistent.
	
4. **Memory Echoes** are used to reveal lore — through visions, interactive objects, or flashbacks.

---

### 🪔 **Summoning Temples & Respawning**

1. **Brazier Mechanic**: If a player dies near a temple or altar, they wake up at the brazier in the center of the location.
2. **Empty Ones**: When a player dies and respawns, their old body becomes an “Empty One” — a zombie-like enemy filled with hatred toward its original.
3. **Empty Ones use the same stats as the PC** but with additional unique attacks or traits.
4. **Respawning only works at specific summoning points** — the world has multiple temples, altars, and other respawn anchors.

---

### 🏰 **World Travel & Navigation**

1. **Paths influence encounter types and puzzles**, not the main story outcome.
2. **Carvings in Stone**: Following ancient carved routes leads to more dangerous areas, as they were abandoned for a reason.
3. **Short-distance travel may use ‘Huts’** that are disguised _tiny hut_ spells. These appear the same inside and may eventually allow teleportation between visited areas.
4. **No true death unless away from a summoning anchor.** Else, the cycle of death and rebirth continues, allowing surreal gameplay loops.

---

### 🧱 **Design & Gameplay Structure**

1. **Each session/area** typically contains:
	
	- 1 puzzle
		
	- 1 encounter
		
	- Optional unique feature or twist
